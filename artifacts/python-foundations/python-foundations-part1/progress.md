### What comes next

The next chapter follows the Learning Path in order: **Chapter 6: Core Collections**. You have already met small lists in Chapter 5 (for example in `enumerate` and `zip`), and Chapter 6 teaches lists, tuples, sets and dictionaries properly. Virtual environments, which you may have heard about, come in Chapter 10, and variable scope comes with functions in Chapter 7. Error handling with `try` and `except`, which the Score Analyser hinted at, comes in Chapter 8.
